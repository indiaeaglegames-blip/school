Frequently asked questions

Do I need a school lab or special equipment?

No. The virtual activities run in a browser. They are simplified learning models and do not replace teacher-supervised practical work.

Can I use the website on a phone?

Yes. The site is responsive and its forms and lab controls are designed for touch screens as well as computers.

Where are my progress, suggestions, and reports saved?

They are stored in local browser storage on this device. They do not sync to another device or reach the project team.

How do I contact support directly?

Use the Email the project team button or email djs07760@gmail.com. Your email app must send the message; this site cannot confirm delivery.

What should I include in a problem report?

Describe the page or activity, what you tried, and what you expected. Do not include passwords or sensitive personal information.

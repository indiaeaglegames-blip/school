For teachers and facilitators

Make the model a class conversation

Use the labs as a demonstration, a paired activity, or a starting point for a discussion with real-world examples.

Before the activity

  • Ask students to predict the outcome.
  • Invite them to name the variable they will change.
  • Connect the topic to a familiar local example.

During the activity

  • Change one control at a time.
  • Ask students to record the reading before resetting.
  • Compare results between pairs or groups.

Discuss together

  • What pattern did you notice?
  • What does this model leave out?
  • How could we investigate this with available materials?
Teacher note: these are simplified educational models. They support discussion and practice; they do not replace a teacher’s instructions or supervised hands-on laboratory safety.
About this project

Learning by observing and trying

URX96 Virtual Labs

A lightweight learning platform for high school students to explore Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Mathematics, and History through interactive models. The site uses HTML, CSS, and JavaScript; it needs no login, paid API, or external images. Experiment notes, quiz scores, and progress are stored locally in this browser.

Core content works offline when this file has been downloaded. Use the controls to explore, record results, and discuss what a model can and cannot show.
